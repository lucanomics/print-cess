be a dated institutional decision with an owner. This file is not permission to deploy.

## Risk ownership

Assign named owners before Production:

- application/security engineering: R-01, R-03, R-07, R-09, R-12, R-15, R-16, R-19, R-20, R-23,
  R-24;
- Windows/device management: R-02, R-04, R-10, R-11, R-21, R-22;
- privacy/legal/procurement: R-04, R-05, R-08, R-12, R-13, R-14, R-25;
- site operations/content/accessibility: R-05, R-06, R-10, R-17, R-18.

If no owner accepts a high or critical residual risk, the service remains unavailable. Risks are
reviewed after any crypto/protocol, parser/renderer, provider, Windows, printer/driver, physical
layout, translation, or operations change and at least quarterly during service.
